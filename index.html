About

Seven floors above Sirkeci

Pino Gare is the rooftop of the Orient Express Hotel, next to the old Sirkeci station. The terrace looks over the Bosphorus, the Golden Horn and Hagia Sophia.

The kitchen cooks seafood, charcoal grill and meze from Turkish and Mediterranean tables. Lunch starts at 13:00. For sunset, book the terrace edge ahead.
